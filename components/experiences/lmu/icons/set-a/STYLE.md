# Set A — Editorial Line

- Grid/viewBox: 24 × 24; geometry aligns primarily to whole pixels.
- Stroke: 1.6, square caps, miter joins; no fills except where essential.
- Corners: crisp and minimal; 1-unit optical clearance at intersections.
- Padding: minimum 3 units from the viewBox edge.
- Motif: direct geometric symbols with open negative space and no enclosing badge.
- Shared Holland metaphors: paired gears, conversation/people, organized checklist,
  palette and brush, podium and microphone, and magnifier with detective hat.
