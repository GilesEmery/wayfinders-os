# Set D — Wayfinding Emblems

- Grid/viewBox: 24 × 24; icons center inside a strong geometric silhouette.
- Stroke: 1.5, round caps and joins; restrained solid micro-marks allowed.
- Corners: emblematic circles, diamonds, hexagons, and stars.
- Padding: minimum 2.5 units around the outer emblem.
- Motif: compact wayfinding marks designed to remain legible at small sizes.
- Shared Holland metaphors: paired gears, conversation/people, organized checklist,
  palette and brush, podium and microphone, and magnifier with detective hat.
